# Judging criteria

Source: [Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/), 13 Nov 2026. Each criterion is scored **1 to 5** at every level of judging. Judges mostly read the project page.

| Criterion | What a 5 looks like (paraphrased) | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | Addresses a significant problem; wide reach; could change things | Tie every insight to crew safety at Moon Base oxygen levels |
| **Creativity** | A new approach, not a small step on existing ideas | More than a search box: something PSI itself does not show |
| **Validity** | Scientifically sound, technically feasible, complete, usable | No invented numbers; every claim traceable to PSI; a working, friendly app |
| **Relevance** | Directly answers the challenge; NASA data is **core**, not decoration | Summarize, rank, interpret - all three; PSI data drives every view |
| **Presentation** | Clear, engaging story of challenge, solution, significance | Judged on the demo **and** the full project page |

The guide warns teams neglect Presentation.

## Stages
1. Universal Event judges pick **Global Nominees** (3 Dec 2026). We are compared only with other Universal Event projects.
2. Global judges pick **Global Finalists** and Honorable Mentions (17 Dec 2026).
3. Executive committee picks 10 **Global Winners** (26 Jan 2027).

## Awards that fit this project
- **Best Use of Data** - makes space data accessible or uses it in a unique way
- **Best Use of Science** - best and most valid use of the scientific method
- **Best Use of Technology** - most innovative use of technology
