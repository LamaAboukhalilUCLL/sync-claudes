Describe a crewed environment. The dashboard ranks NASA's microgravity combustion investigations by how closely their test conditions match it, explains why, and shows where the data runs out. Every investigation links to its record and DOI in NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) repository.

Scenario

Score = need 30 + fuel type 25 + oxygen 25 + pressure 10 + air flow 10. Ground studies lose 5. Oxygen, pressure and flow score full marks when the scenario value lies inside the range the investigation tested, and fall off with distance outside it.
