# Submission checklist

Form fields, from the Project Submission Guide and [[Notes about project guide]].

- [ ] **Project name**
- [ ] **High-level summary** (50-200 words): what we developed, how it addresses the challenge, why it is important
- [ ] **Link to demo** (30 s video or 7 slides or fewer; public, no login)
- [ ] **Link to final project** (public repo or website, no login)
- [ ] **Detailed description:** what it does and how it works; benefits; intended impact; tools, languages, software; how it is creative; factors considered (energy, cost, accessibility)
- [ ] **Use of SI** - from [[SI use log]]
- [ ] **NASA data** - link text and URL for each, from [[References]]
- [ ] **Space Agency Partner and other data** - everything external, from [[References]]
- [ ] Tick Terms and Conditions; tick "all work is original"
- [ ] **Submit for Judging** - first version Sunday afternoon, final before 23:59

## Before submitting
- [ ] At least 2 members on the Members tab, all in the Universal Event
- [ ] Every link opens in a private browser window
- [ ] Everything in English
- [ ] AI-generated images or video carry a visible watermark
- [ ] DOI shown for every investigation in the app
- [ ] Demo within the limit
